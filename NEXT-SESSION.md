# The Inner Ring — Next Session

Open <https://clangky.github.io/inner-ring/> on a preferred desktop browser and
spend several minutes with only these questions:

1. Does the space feel like the inside of a continuous ring rather than a
   generic tunnel?
2. Do the front and back membranes feel meaningfully different near contact?
3. Does changing Spine alter the bodily character of the experience?
4. Is the autonomous motion compelling at its default speed, or merely busy?

Then repeat briefly on a phone. Record the felt response before requesting new
controls. The most useful next iteration should sharpen the central experience,
not widen the feature set.
