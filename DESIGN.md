# The Inner Ring — Design Grammar

## The spatial proposition

The observer is not outside a torus looking at an object. The observer occupies
the hollow center of the torus's tube and initially faces across that tube's
diameter. Forward and backward therefore lead toward two membranes rather than
along an endless corridor. The larger ring still curves continuously around the
observer, visible through peripheral perspective, particles, and axial strands.

This resolves the original ambiguity intentionally: the ring is both a place
and a boundary, but never a diagram shown from a safe distance.

## What moves

The wall is generated parametrically in a GPU vertex shader. Several wave
fields cross the torus at incompatible rates. Their combined displacement is
quantized into terraces, then intersected by a variable number of gear-like
teeth. The result should feel as though an unseen exterior medium continually
presses the ring into temporary forms.

The fragment shader gives those forms an interference texture rather than a
painted skin. Color comes from the same changing quantities that deform the
geometry, so the wall's appearance and shape remain causally related.

## The observer's spine

Spine is not a cosmetic control. It changes three coupled things:

1. the observer's vertical position and roll;
2. the horizon and pitch of perception;
3. the asymmetric bias through which exterior pressure deforms the wall.

The control therefore treats posture as one of the simulation's boundary
conditions. The field is not wholly independent of the body witnessing it.

## Contact

Near either movement limit, the nearest membrane receives a distinct chromatic
focus and a screen-space veil. The front tends toward hot rose; the back tends
toward cold cyan. This is not meant to claim that the two boundaries are
metaphysically different. It gives the user a felt orientation inside a space
that would otherwise be perfectly symmetrical.

## Intentional limits

- This is an experiential artwork, not a scientific model of perception.
- There is no score, win condition, or prescribed interpretation.
- Audio is omitted in the first version so the encounter can remain quiet and
  immediately accessible without a browser permission gesture.
- The first public version favors a single strong spatial proposition over a
  collection of camera modes.
